## Hi, I'm Shuban

That said, most of the writeups on this website cover challenges from past **[Capture The Flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capture_the_flag_(cybersecurity))** (CTF) cybersecurity competitions that I competed in with my team, **[Cosmic Bit Flip](https://ctftime.org/team/372043)**, my solutions to past competitive programming competitions, and possibly some uncategorized cs/math puzzles. As a new college student, I have started through my journey competing in the ICPC competition which I hope to document here.

Some of my hobbies include taking long walks with no real destination, studying math and physics without the stress of a grade, and improving my cooking skills.
